x402.NanoSession Rev8 + RaiFlow + xno-skills tx. Deterministic per-session micropayments, payment detection, and agentic Nano settlement (feeless, sub-second).

## What it is

RaiFlow and the x402 NanoSession work explore how instant, feeless Nano payments can protect APIs, settle sessions, and make machine-to-machine payment flows viable.

## Why it matters

HTTP 402 patterns are usually demonstrated with high-friction payment rails. Nano changes the economics: no fee floor, fast confirmation, and direct settlement semantics.

## Current angle

This content should become the bridge between standards work and demos: enough narrative for humans, enough detail for implementers.
