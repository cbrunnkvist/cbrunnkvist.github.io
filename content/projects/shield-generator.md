OWS-secured key custody, policy-gated signing & xno-skills agent runtime. Zero-fee Nano wallet ops with MCP/CLI for AI agents and humans.

## What it is

`xno-skills` packages Nano wallet operations for agentic workflows: wallet discovery, balances, sends, receives, QR generation, message signing, RPC queries, and MCP/CLI access.

## Why it matters

Most wallet tooling assumes a human clicking through a browser. This project makes Nano operations scriptable and agent-friendly while keeping key custody local through OWS.
